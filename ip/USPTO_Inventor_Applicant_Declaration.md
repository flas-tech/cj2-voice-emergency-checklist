## B. Entity Status — the critical decision (affects fee AND validity)

The provisional fee depends on entity status. Current USPTO fees (verify on uspto.gov before filing — they change annually):

| Status | Provisional fee (37 CFR 1.16(d)) | Extra form needed |
|---|---|---|
| Large entity | **$320** | none |
| **Small entity** | **$160** | none (just check the box) |
| **Micro entity** | **$65** | **Form PTO/SB/15A** (gross-income basis) |

### Why this is NOT simple for an LLC applicant

Micro-entity status is **not** based only on the inventor's income. When an **LLC is the applicant/assignee**, the gross-income test applies to the **LLC itself** as well as to the inventor:

- **Small-entity requirement:** the LLC must qualify as a small entity (≤500 employees, and not have assigned/licensed rights to a non-small entity). For a small startup LLC this is usually fine.
- **Four-application limit:** neither Michael Gravalec nor the LLC may have been named on **more than 4** prior **non-provisional** U.S. applications (provisionals, PCT, and prior-employer-assigned apps don't count). **[CONFIRM — has Michael been named on >4 prior nonprovisionals?]**
- **Gross-income limit (~$251,190 for 2026 — verify current figure):** this must be satisfied by **BOTH**:
  - the **inventor** (Michael Gravalec — his personal gross income in the preceding calendar year), **and**
  - the **applicant LLC** (Flite Line Aviation Services, LLC — its own gross income in the preceding calendar year), **and**
  - any entity the rights are assigned/obligated to.
- If the LLC's gross income exceeds the limit, **micro entity is NOT available** even if Michael personally qualifies.

### Recommendation

- If you are **not sure** the LLC's gross income is below ~$251,190 for the preceding calendar year, **file as SMALL ENTITY ($160).** This is the safe choice: improperly claiming micro entity can be treated as a **fee deficiency** and, if done in bad faith, can raise inequitable-conduct concerns. The $95 difference is not worth the risk.
- File as **MICRO ENTITY ($65)** only if you can affirmatively certify (on Form SB/15A) that **all** of: small-entity status, the 4-application limit, Michael's personal income, AND the LLC's income are within limits.

**[DECIDE — Small ($160, no extra form, safe) vs Micro ($65, requires SB/15A + LLC income certification).]**
