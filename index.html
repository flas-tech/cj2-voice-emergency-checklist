How this works
  1. Hold Push-to-Talk and say the emergency name (e.g. “engine fire”). Release to submit.
  2. The system reads the matching checklist aloud, one item at a time.
  3. After each item, hold Push-to-Talk and say the item’s completion word (shown under each step), or a universal word like “check”, “complete”, or “next”.
  4. It advances to the next item and reads it. Repeat until the checklist is complete.
  5. Say “reset” (or tap Reset) to start over. No internet or login needed.

Troubleshooting
  • Button does nothing / no mic: Use Chrome or Edge on desktop and allow microphone access when prompted. Safari and Firefox have limited support.
  • Must be HTTPS or localhost: The mic only works on a secure (https://) page or on localhost. A plain file:// page won’t get mic access.
  • Nothing is recognized: Speak clearly after the button turns red, and keep holding until you finish the phrase.
  • No voice / silent readout: Check system volume and that the tab isn’t muted. Tap any button once to satisfy the browser’s audio-autoplay rule.
  • Wrong checklist matched: Use the exact emergency name, or tap its chip instead.
  • Always use the buttons (Mark Complete / Repeat / Reset) as a fallback if voice misfires.
